Community Guidelines & Member Etiquette

InclusiveMatch AI was built to foster an authentic, safe, and empowering space for persons with disabilities, the Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing community, and genuine allies. These guidelines establish the standards of conduct expected of every member.

Our Guiding Principles

Treat every person with dignity and respect. Respect different communication modalities—whether Indian Sign Language, text, or speech. Never cross boundaries or coerce others. Prioritize mutual safety and authentic connection above all else.

1. The Five Pillars of Our Community

  1. Radical Inclusion & Accessibility: We celebrate neurological, physical, and sensory diversity. Respect other members' communication pacing, assistive tools, and preferred communication modes (Sign Language, Text, Speech-to-Text).
  2. Uncompromising Consent: All communication must be consensual. No member is obligated to match, respond, accept a call, or share personal details. "No" always means "no".
  3. Authenticity & Honesty: Present yourself truthfully. We encourage verified profile badges through live face verification to ensure an environment free of catfishing and deceptive profiles.
  4. Zero Tolerance for Abuse: Harassment, threats, ableist insults, and discrimination have no place in our community.
  5. Kindness in Parting: Connections do not always work out. Part ways gracefully, without hostility, passive aggression, or vindictive behavior.

2. Prohibited Behaviors

The following behaviors result in immediate warning strikes, temporary restrictions, or permanent account bans:

Threats & Violence

Any threat of physical harm, battery, stalking, extortion, or intimidation. Masking violent threats behind emojis or "just kidding" is strictly caught and penalized by our zero-tolerance safety rules.

Harassment & Bullying

Repeated unsolicited messaging after being told to stop, name-calling, belittling, or using alternate accounts to contact someone who restricted or parted ways with you.

Hate Speech & Ableism

Disparaging, mocking, or insulting anyone based on disability, medical condition, caste, religion, race, gender identity, or sexual orientation.

Scams & Commercial Solicitation

Asking for money, gift cards, investments, cryptocurrency, or promoting external products, services, or commercial webcam links.

4. How Automated NLP Moderation Works

InclusiveMatch AI utilizes an automated Natural Language Processing moderation engine to protect members from abusive text in real-time:

  • Context-Aware Intelligence: Our system is trained to distinguish harmless jokes, gaming hyperbole (e.g. "I'm going to kill you in Ludo 🎮"), and friendly teasing from genuine hostility.
  • Automated Warnings: If an outgoing message is classified as abusive, threatening, or harassing, the message is blocked from delivery, and a warning is logged.
  • Automated 7-Day Restriction: Receiving 5 warnings automatically restricts the sender's account for 7 days.
  • Probabilistic Nature: AI models make estimates and can occasionally produce false positives or false negatives. An AI classification score is an operational safeguard, not definitive proof of bad intent.

5. Investigation & Admin Actions

When a report is filed, our human administrative team reviews the incident. Administrators have the authority to take the following actions under platform policy:

  • Warning Issued: An administrative warning is applied to the reported user's record.
  • Message Removed: Offending content is deleted from active database records.
  • One-Week Account Block: The account is locked for 7 days (is_active=False with block_expiry).
  • Permanent Account Deactivation: For severe violations (extortion, physical threats, illegal material, repeat harassment), the account is permanently deactivated.
  • Dismissal: If the report is determined to be unfounded or filed in bad faith, it is dismissed without penalty.

6. Appeals & Manual Review Process

If you believe a warning was issued in error or that an automated 7-day restriction was triggered by an algorithmic false positive, you have the right to request a human administrative appeal:

  1. Navigate to the Contact & Grievance Portal.
  2. Select the category "Privacy Complaint or Violation" or "General Legal / Moderation Inquiry".
  3. Provide your registered email address, approximate timestamp of the incident, and a brief explanation of why the message was a false positive or benign context.
  4. Our administrative team will review the flagged message snippet and logs. If a false positive is confirmed, the warning strike will be expunged and any restriction lifted.
