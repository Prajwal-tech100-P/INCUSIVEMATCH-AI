Privacy Policy & Notice of Digital Personal Data Processing

InclusiveMatch AI operates on a "Privacy-by-Design" architecture. This document discloses our verified data handling practices under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

Plain-Language Privacy Summary
  • We do not record video or audio calls: Live video/audio streams are transmitted directly peer-to-peer (or via relays) using secure encrypted protocols. Our servers never record, record-store, or tap media streams.
  • We do not store sign language camera frames: Live video frames submitted for assistive sign language recognition are processed in-memory for landmark estimation and immediately discarded.
  • Messages are not end-to-end encrypted: Private messages are stored securely in our encrypted database so you and your matches can read conversation history, and are analyzed in real-time by automated moderation to protect members from threats and harassment.
  • No data selling or third-party behavioral ads: We do not sell your personal data or deploy third-party advertising trackers.

3. Communication & Media Processing

Real-Time Audio & Video Calls

InclusiveMatch AI supports peer-to-peer real-time video communication for accessible dating:

  • Peer-to-Peer Transmission: Video and audio streams are exchanged directly between participant browsers using secure real-time protocols with end-to-end transport encryption.
  • No Call Recording: Our server acts solely as a signaling intermediary to connect participant browsers. Our backend does not record, tap, transcode, or save any audio or video stream.
  • Call Metadata: We store minimal signaling session records (caller ID, recipient ID, call status, timestamp). These records expire automatically upon call conclusion or expiration.

Indian Sign Language (ISL) Video Frame Processing

When you enable real-time sign language recognition during a video call:

  • Your browser captures single video frames from your local webcam and transmits them over secure HTTPS to the recognition service.
  • The server runs real-time computer vision landmark analysis to evaluate sign language sequences in volatile memory.
  • Immediate Frame Disposal: The received raw image buffer is processed strictly in volatile RAM and is discarded immediately after landmark classification. Camera frames are never written to disk or stored in persistent databases.

Speech-to-Text (STT) and Text-to-Speech (TTS)

Spoken communication accessibility uses the browser's native Web Speech API (SpeechRecognition and SpeechSynthesis). Audio capture and synthesis are executed locally inside your web browser. Transcribed subtitle text is relayed ephemerally to your matched partner during the active call session and is not logged on the server.

4. AI Models & Algorithmic Processing

InclusiveMatch AI uses artificial intelligence exclusively for accessibility enhancement, compatibility discovery, and member protection:

  • AI Compatibility Recommender: Automated compatibility matching evaluates profile affinity based on mutual interests, communication preference overlap, and age preferences. Recommendation scores are ephemeral heuristics; we do not employ automated decision-making that produces legal consequences.
  • Safety Moderation: Outgoing chat messages are screened in real-time by an automated natural language moderation engine assessing six safety categories (toxic, severe_toxic, insult, threat, harassment, hate). The system applies context-aware rules to protect friendly banter, gaming idioms, and playful teasing while strictly blocking genuine threats.
  • Face Verification: Users can optionally verify their profile photo using automated facial comparison to ensure profile photo authenticity. The live webcam capture is compared with the reference profile photo in-memory, and only a verification status flag is recorded in the user profile.

5. Infrastructure & Third-Party Processors

We work exclusively with reputable cloud infrastructure providers that maintain strict industry certifications (ISO 27001, SOC 2):

  • Managed Cloud Database: Enterprise cloud database infrastructure hosting personal profile records, encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Cloudinary: Persistent cloud media storage for profile photos. Uploaded images are delivered via Cloudinary's secure HTTPS CDN. When you delete a photo or account, it is deleted from Cloudinary via its management API.

  • STUN / TURN Servers: Secure STUN/TURN servers used exclusively for NAT traversal to establish direct peer-to-peer WebRTC connections.

7. Your Data Subject Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

Under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you possess the following enforceable statutory rights:

  1. Right to Access Information (Section 11): You have the right to obtain a summary of your personal data processed by us and the identities of data processors. You can download an instant, machine-readable JSON copy of your personal data directly from your Data Rights Dashboard.
  2. Right to Correction & Updating (Section 12): You can correct, update, or complete inaccurate personal data at any time via the Edit Profile interface or by submitting a Data Correction request.
  3. Right to Erasure (Section 12): You have the right to request the erasure of your personal data unless retention is mandated by law. You can execute an automated, permanent account deletion from your account settings.
  4. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): You have the right to readily available means of grievance redressal regarding any violation of your privacy rights.
  5. Right to Nominate (Section 14): You have the right to nominate an individual who, in the event of death or incapacity, shall exercise your data rights.

8. Data Security & Storage Architecture

We deploy rigorous technical and organizational security measures to protect your digital personal data:

  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; we utilize industry-standard cryptographic hashing with unique per-user salts.
  • HTTPS & Transport Encryption: All client-server traffic is strictly encrypted using TLS 1.3/1.2 with HSTS preloading enabled in production.
  • Session Hardening: Authentication cookies are configured with HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure flags to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) and cross-site request forgery (CSRF).
  • Atomic Warning Deduplication: Concurrency controls and database deduplication safeguard safety warnings across multi-threaded workers.
