User Safety & Dating Protection Policy

Your safety, dignity, and autonomy are our highest priorities. This guide outlines the protective features built directly into InclusiveMatch AI and recommended safety practices for online and real-world interactions.

Core Safety Rules
  • Never send money: Legitimate matches will never ask for financial assistance, UPI transfers, bank details, or gift cards.
  • Keep conversations on-platform: Stay on InclusiveMatch AI until you have established mutual trust. Our built-in NLP moderation and calling safeguards only protect you while on the platform.
  • Verify your match: Look for the purple Verified Badge () indicating authentic selfie verification, and use our WebRTC video calls to verify who you are talking to before meeting.

1. In-App Protective Features

InclusiveMatch AI incorporates multiple defensive layers to ensure a secure dating environment:

Biometric Selfie Face Verification

Our automated face verification tool securely matches a live camera capture with profile photos, stopping impersonation and awarding verified badges.

Automated NLP Toxicity Screening

Every message is screened in real-time. Uncompromising physical battery threats are immediately blocked. Accumulating 5 warning strikes triggers an automated 7-day restriction.

Instant Bilateral Restriction

The "Restrict User" button instantly cuts all communication ties, cancels active matches, and prevents the restricted user from contacting or finding you anywhere on the platform.

Location Privacy Guard

Your precise GPS coordinates are never displayed on your public profile. Only your city and state text representation is visible to prospective matches.

2. Online Communication Safety

When interacting with new connections:

  • Protect Personally Identifiable Information: Avoid sharing your home address, workplace, national identification numbers (Aadhaar, PAN), or exact financial status.
  • Be Mindful of Phishing Links: Do not open unverified external links or download unfamiliar files sent to you in chat.
  • Video Call Verification: Before considering meeting someone in person, conduct an accessible video call through our platform to verify their appearance, demeanor, and identity.

3. Financial Scam & Romance Fraud Defense

Fraudsters frequently invent urgent crises to solicit money. Remember:

  • Never transfer money or share banking details: Under no circumstances should you wire money, share UPI PINs, transfer cryptocurrency, or purchase gift cards for someone you met online.
  • Watch for Emotional Manipulation: Be alert to individuals who declare intense romantic feelings prematurely, claim to have an emergency medical crisis, or claim to be stranded abroad.
  • Report Immediately: If anyone asks you for money, immediately use the Report User button and select "Scams & Financial Solicitation".

4. In-Person Meeting Safety Protocol

When you decide to meet a match offline, observe these critical safety precautions:

  1. Meet in a Well-Lit, Public Space: Choose a bustling public café, accessible restaurant, or public venue. Never agree to meet in private residences, hotel rooms, or secluded outdoor locations on early dates.
  2. Arrange Your Own Transportation: Travel to and from the date independently. Do not let your date pick you up from your residence or know where you live until deep mutual trust is established.
  3. Inform a Trusted Friend or Family Member: Share details of your date (the person's profile, venue name, arrival time) with a friend. Arrange a scheduled check-in call or message.
  4. Keep Your Mobile Phone Charged: Ensure your phone has full battery and active cellular data for emergency communication.
  5. Stay in Control: If you feel uncomfortable, pressured, or unsafe at any point, end the date immediately and leave. Your comfort and safety take precedence over politeness.

5. Accessible Safety Considerations

For members with disabilities, deaf or hard-of-hearing members, and neurodivergent individuals:

  • Accessible Meeting Locations: Ensure the chosen venue meets your accessibility requirements (wheelchair ramps, appropriate ambient lighting for sign language or lip-reading, quiet acoustic spaces for hearing aids).
  • Communication Backup: If using Indian Sign Language or speech-to-text, ensure you have accessible mobile communication backup tools ready.
  • Sensory Safety: Choose environments that do not overwhelm your sensory comfort levels.
