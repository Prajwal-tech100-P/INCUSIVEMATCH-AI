1. Your Five Statutory Data Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 guarantees you the following enforceable rights as a Data Principal:

  1. Right to Access Summary of Personal Data (Section 11): You may request a clear summary of all digital personal data processed by InclusiveMatch AI. You can generate an instant, machine-readable JSON data export directly from your account.
  2. Right to Correction & Updating (Section 12): You may correct inaccurate or misleading personal data, complete incomplete data, or update your information at any time.
  3. Right to Erasure (Section 12): You have the right to demand the erasure of your personal data when it is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected.
  4. Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13): You have the right to readily accessible grievance resolution mechanisms provided by our Data Protection Grievance Officer.
  5. Right to Nominate (Section 14): You have the right to designate an individual who shall exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

2. How to Delete Your Account

InclusiveMatch AI provides an authenticated, self-service account deletion workflow directly within your profile settings:

  1. Log into your InclusiveMatch AI account.
  2. Navigate to your Profile Page and scroll to the Privacy & Account Management section.
  3. Click on "Delete My Account" to open the security confirmation modal.
  4. Enter your current password to verify your identity and prevent accidental or unauthorized deletion.
  5. Confirm deletion. The system immediately executes the backend cleanup protocol and logs you out.

4. Legitimate Legal & Safety Retention Exceptions

In strict compliance with Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act, 2023 and Rule 3(1)(h) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021, certain minimal records are retained for legitimate safety and legal purposes:

  • Abuse Reports & Investigation Records: If an account is the subject of an unresolved safety complaint, sexual harassment report, or cybercrime investigation, records in safety reports archives are preserved in secure, access-restricted administrator archives. This prevents abusive bad actors from evading accountability simply by deleting their profile.
  • Banned Perpetrator Hashes: For users who have been permanently banned for severe threats or illegal conduct, an encrypted, non-reversible cryptographic hash of the email address is maintained in an exclusion register to prevent ban circumvention.
  • Statutory Preservation Period: Safety audit records are maintained strictly for [DATA_RETENTION_PERIOD_DAYS, e.g., 180 days] as required by Indian intermediary regulations, after which they are permanently expunged.

5. Requesting Data Correction or Erasure

If you prefer not to use self-service account deletion, or if you need to submit a formal statutory request regarding your personal data:

  1. Visit our Contact & Grievance Portal.
  2. Select the relevant category:
    • "Data Access Request (DPDP Act)"
    • "Data Correction or Updating (DPDP Act)"
    • "Data Deletion / Erasure Request"
  3. Provide your registered email address and detailed instructions.
  4. Our Grievance Redressal team will authenticate your identity and fulfill your request within the statutory timeframe (acknowledgment within 24 hours; completion within 15 days).

6. Nomination of Data Rights Representative

Under Section 14 of the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to nominate any individual who shall, in the event of death or incapacity, exercise your data rights. To register a nomination, submit a formal request via the Grievance Portal detailing your registered account information and the full legal name and contact details of your designated nominee.
